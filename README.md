# 📊 Sales Analysis 2024/2025

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance across 2024 and 2025 for a distribution/FMCG product portfolio (Nagiko, Ric-Giko, Tomagood, Erisco brands).

---

## 🔑 Key Results
| Metric | 2024 | 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Sales | ₦14.98bn | ₦18.33bn | ▲ ~22% |
| Total Quantity Sold | 1.05M | 1.22M | ▲ ~16% |
| Average Price per CTN | ₦14.30K | ₦14.98K | ▲ ~5% |

**Top Product (both years):** Nagiko 400G Tin consistently led sales volume, followed by Ric-Giko and Tomagood variants.
